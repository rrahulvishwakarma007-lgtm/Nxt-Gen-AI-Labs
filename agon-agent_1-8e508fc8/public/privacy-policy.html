Our WhatsApp automation service is built on the official Meta WhatsApp Business API. By messaging our WhatsApp number, your interaction is also subject to Meta's Privacy Policy at whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Message logs are stored on secure servers for up to 90 days to monitor bot performance, then automatically deleted.

We only send messages in response to messages you initiate. We do not send unsolicited promotional messages.

Access to stored data is restricted to authorised NXT Gen AI Labs personnel only. We do not store payment information.
